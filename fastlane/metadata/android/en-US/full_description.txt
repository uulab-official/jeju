Sorang Jeju brings Jeju activities, destinations, maps, history, culture, and language together in one app. Sorang means love in the Jeju language.

Explore places and activities by theme, use guides built from official tourism sources to shape your day, and save destinations for later. Follow a concise history timeline from Tamna through Jeju 4·3 and peace. The app also includes everyday dialect, proverbs, dictionaries, indexed reference words, and the Jeju orthography guide.

Key features
- Discover nature, beaches, walks, culture, markets, islands, and activities
- Themed guides for eastern Jeju, active trips, rainy days, and sunsets
- Jeju history timeline with official sources
- Place highlights, official sources, and map links
- Save destinations and Jeju words
- Unified search across four public Jeju language datasets
- Meanings, pronunciation, orthography, and offline cache
- Jeju news notifications and system, light, and dark themes

Tourism sources: Korea Tourism Organization TourAPI and Visit Jeju
History sources: National Museum of Jeju, Heritage Portal, and Jeju 4·3 Peace Foundation
Language source: Jeju Special Self-Governing Province OpenAPI
